Day by day

  1. Dec 18 Fri

    ✈️ Depart Chicago

    Wheels up from O'Hare (ORD), overnight flight across the Pacific. Lose a day crossing the International Date Line — land Dec 19.

  2. Dec 19 Sat

    🗼 Arrive Tokyo (night)

    Land in Tokyo late in the evening. Just enough time to get to the hotel, grab a late bite (konbini runs count), and crash — early flight to Sapporo the next morning.

  3. Dec 20 Sun

    ❄️ Fly to Sapporo

    Morning flight from Tokyo up to Sapporo (New Chitose). Spend the rest of the day in the city — Odori Park, Sapporo Beer Museum, Nijo Market, ramen alley, and the White Illumination lights if they're up.

  4. Dec 21 Mon

    ♨️ Noboribetsu

    Head to Noboribetsu, Hokkaido's biggest onsen resort town. Walk through Jigokudani ("Hell Valley"), soak in a ryokan onsen, and warm up from the Hokkaido cold.

  5. Dec 22 Tue

    🌃 Hakodate

    On to Hakodate. Morning market for fresh seafood, Goryokaku fort, the old red-brick warehouses, and the famous night view from Mt. Hakodate.

  6. Dec 23 Wed

    ✈️ Tokyo → Hong Kong

    Fly back down to Tokyo, then same-day connection onward to Hong Kong (HKG). Long travel day — tight-ish connection, so no dawdling at the gate.

Good to know

Pack for the cold

Hokkaido in December is genuinely cold and snowy — heavy coat, thermal layers, waterproof/insulated boots, gloves, hand warmers. Tokyo will feel mild by comparison.

Tight connections

Dec 20 (Tokyo→Sapporo) and Dec 23 (Tokyo→Hong Kong) are same-day connections. Keep carry-ons light and don't linger — build in buffer where you can.

Onsen etiquette

Noboribetsu is all about the onsen — wash thoroughly before soaking, no swimsuits, tattoos may need to be covered depending on the ryokan.

Ground transport

Sapporo → Noboribetsu → Hakodate is doable by train/limited express or bus — worth locking in tickets ahead of the holidays since this is peak season.
