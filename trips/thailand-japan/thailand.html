Good to know

Long Frankfurt layover

The outbound routing via Frankfurt on Condor (DE2017 / DE2368) included a 9-hour layover — enough time to leave the airport if needed.

Thai AirAsia domestic hops

Both internal Thailand flights — Phuket → Chiang Mai (FD3159) and Chiang Mai → Bangkok (FD3438) — were on Thai AirAsia.

Hotel switches mid-stay

Phuket split between Novotel Phuket City Phokeethra and Paradox Resort Phuket; Bangkok split between Jubilee Prestige Hotel Ratchadapisek and KITZIO HOUSE HOTEL.
