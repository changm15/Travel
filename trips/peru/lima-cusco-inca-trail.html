Good to know

Altitude

Cusco sits around 11,000+ ft — the Sep 3–4 stretch is built in to acclimatize before the trek starts.

Inca Trail permits

The classic Inca Trail requires a permit that regularly sells out months ahead — worth double-checking booking status is locked in.

Pack for the trek

Layers for cold nights and warm days, broken-in boots, and a rain layer even in the dry season.

Lima bookend

Sep 7–8 in Lima is unstructured — good window for Miraflores or Barranco, or just resting up before the flight home.
